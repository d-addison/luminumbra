Re-seal note, 2026-10-11

What changed
  Five receipts recorded the machine-local build root of the isolated
  campaign (a WSL UNC path and a WSL-style absolute path) in their "path"
  fields. Every such prefix was replaced with the neutral placeholder
  <build-root>; the remainder of each path (authoring-isolated/campaign-
  20260913/...) is unchanged. The randomly named per-run temporary roots
  (C:/Temp/luminumbra-viewport-checks-<32 hex>) in the two process receipts
  were likewise replaced with <temp-root>. Standard tool install locations
  (the Blender and MSYS2 Python executables) contain no user-specific data
  and were left as recorded. No measurement, identity, source hash, case
  result or timestamp was altered.

Why
  The repository owner directed the redaction of local filesystem paths from
  published evidence on 2026-10-11. Because receipt.json and two other
  receipts are pinned by SHA-256 in sibling files, those pins were recomputed
  from the redacted files.
