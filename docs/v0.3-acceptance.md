## Saved worlds

The catalog now performs complete validation on one background worker, coalesces
refreshes into one pending request, cancels obsolete work and publishes results
only on the UI thread. The selector displays a pending state and disables loading
until validation completes. World opening still validates again before replacing
the current world; supported formats and corruption checks are unchanged.

A Release diagnostic against the preserved approximately 177 MB, 5,433-chunk save
measured an 8.25-second synchronous catalog call before this change. With background
validation, the real selector opened in 14.1 ms, processed 6,357 UI updates while
validation took 10.26 seconds, and had a maximum measured update of 23.2 ms. These
are local responsiveness observations, not portable performance budgets.

A separately assembled Linux Release runtime completed create, play, fill two
terrain chunks, save, return to menu, clean process exit, restart, select and load
through the production UI. Loading adopted all 5,433 saved chunks and visibly
restored the edit. All nine saved files retained their pre-restart hashes. Creating
a second world and switching back restored the edit again; all eleven files across
both worlds were unchanged by that load. Packaged obsolete/future/corrupt/missing
metadata, missing preset and truncated-region refusals preserved disk bytes. Saves
that became corrupt or disappeared after catalog validation were refused when
opening; an invalid catalog root displayed an error. All four UI sessions exited
cleanly. A private native `14480a8` package also completed create/edit/save/restart
and second-world switching through the production UI. The restored mound remained
visible and the player grounded. Nine files were unchanged after restart loading;
eleven files across both worlds were unchanged after the final switch. All three
native client sessions exited 0. The second driver reached its bounded lifetime
before switch-back; the third completed that check. These UNC-filesystem runs
establish persistence and support, not a native-disk responsiveness budget or
whole-scene/audio acceptance. Final release-package identity remains required.
The older campaign fixture and its separate preserved copy remain intact.

A version-stamped private native `c6fe309` candidate exposed a separate walking
spawn failure during new-world acceptance. At Default/424242, x=z=8, terrain is
15.202278 m and the camera starts at 17.152279 m. The local controller subtracts
its eye height to pass feet positions, but the physics capsule was centered on
that position, embedding its base 1.3 m below the claimed feet. A physics-only
zero-input reproduction with a real terrain ray hit falls to y=-4276.107 m at
the existing 50 ms frame clamp, at the same x/z as the native failure. Raising
the old capsule by 1.3 m prevents that fall.

The correction translates both local-player shapes to a common feet origin,
retains their dimensions, accepts lower-sphere slope contacts and checks standing
clearance at the standing shape's center of mass. Shape changes use Jolt's
penetration slop and update the client stance only when accepted. Server-avatar
construction and scheduling are unchanged. The regression compares feet against
real collider rays at the player's current x/z, at both 1/60 s and 50 ms.
Repeated stance changes, low-ceiling refusal/recovery and walking on a 45-degree
slope also pass. Independent Astra/xhigh source review accepted this bounded
correction; its six focused Debug checks passed without skips. Native `14480a8`
entry, stance, noclip return and saved-world switching also pass. Final packaging
will include the subsequent native noise correction.

Idle downhill drift remains a separate movement limitation: the corrected shape
follows the same path as the safely spawned old shape to approximately 0.00002 m
after accounting for the 1.3 m origin offset. Existing grounded velocity includes
a constant downward component. That tuning is tracked separately from the spawn
overlap; the regression does not assert stationary idle motion.

The older simplified-spawn test also compared against the original hill height.
A separate before/after probe shows identical horizontal paths for a safely
placed old capsule and the corrected feet-origin capsule; their y positions differ
by 1.3 m. The final feet at y=18.806541 m are supported by a real collider at
18.767416 m. That test now checks the current collider and grounded state with
tighter clearance bounds, preserving its collision-bootstrap requirement.

## Source-release inventory

Independent release-readiness review found that the original SPDX 2.3 inventory
omitted the creation timestamp, required file SHA-1 digests, and analyzed-package
verification code. Its version-only namespace also collided between artifact
profiles. The corrected generator inventories the exact source archive, retains
SHA-256 alongside SHA-1, records the reproducible creation convention, and derives
a unique document identity from its complete content. The source workflow checks
the pinned official schema, inventory semantics and exact archive membership and
digests before attestation. Ordinary engine tests use the standard library and
isolated Git fixtures; schema dependencies belong to release tooling. This is a
file inventory, not a complete dependency or license audit. See
[release tooling](https://github.com/d-addison/luminumbra/blob/devel/tools/release/README.md) and
[issue #119](https://github.com/d-addison/luminumbra/issues/119).

## Remaining release gates and limits

At the user's direction, audio playback is disabled for v0.3 and its private
preview. Normal client startup uses the null backend regardless of persisted
volume, and disabled settings report that state. `--enable-audio` is an explicit
experimental diagnostic opt-in; `--no-audio` always suppresses it. Existing audio
code and private evidence remain available for a separate redesign workstream.
Release acceptance verifies silent startup and truthful controls; it does not
claim acceptable sound design or complete audible-event coverage.

Final committed-candidate checks, applicable native/manual coverage, the unchanged
120-second UCRT64 guard, server-load qualification, paired performance gates,
independent Astra/xhigh verification, strict CI promotion, signed source-only
publication and independent release-download verification remain required.

Finite surface residency, the 3,200 m far plane, finite cave residency and omitted
distant caves/edits remain visible limitations. Cave captures still expose the
finite cave boundary. Audio audibility/event coverage, broader hardware and real
GNS/Steam transport qualification must not be inferred from decoding, software GL
or compile probes. The original intermittent server-load crash retains its own
reproduction and unresolved cause; the separate MSVC exception-configuration fix
does not establish its cause.

See the [roadmap](roadmap.md) and its individual acceptance issues for ownership,
dependencies and closure requirements. Engine/game extraction follows verified
publication and the agreed roadmap approval.
