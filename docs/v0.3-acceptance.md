## Saved worlds

The catalog now performs complete validation on one background worker, coalesces
refreshes into one pending request, cancels obsolete work and publishes results
only on the UI thread. The selector displays a pending state and disables loading
until validation completes. World opening still validates again before replacing
the current world; supported formats and corruption checks are unchanged.

A Release diagnostic against the preserved approximately 177 MB, 5,433-chunk save
measured an 8.25-second synchronous catalog call before this change. With background
validation, the real selector opened in 14.1 ms, processed 6,357 UI updates while
validation took 10.26 seconds, and had a maximum measured update of 23.2 ms. These
are local responsiveness observations, not portable performance budgets.

A separately assembled Linux Release runtime completed create, play, fill two
terrain chunks, save, return to menu, clean process exit, restart, select and load
through the production UI. Loading adopted all 5,433 saved chunks and visibly
restored the edit. All nine saved files retained their pre-restart hashes. Creating
a second world and switching back restored the edit again; all eleven files across
both worlds were unchanged by that load. Packaged obsolete/future/corrupt/missing
metadata, missing preset and truncated-region refusals preserved disk bytes. Saves
that became corrupt or disappeared after catalog validation were refused when
opening; an invalid catalog root displayed an error. All four UI sessions exited
cleanly. Final committed native-package acceptance remains required.
The older campaign fixture and its separate preserved copy remain intact.

## Validation and visual findings

- Full Debug and ASan each passed 1,979 tests with zero skips after the grass,
  glass and catalog corrections. ASan used explicit X11/software GL with leak
  detection enabled. An earlier run accidentally selected Wayland/EGL and reported
  driver leaks; its failure evidence is retained separately.
- Native MSVC focused validation passed 181 tests with zero skips, including the
  active glass pass, grass/weather/ray composition, cloud projection, water/soil
  readback, content refusal, saved-world validation and UI.
- The blocking clang-tidy selection passed all translation units changed since
  `f18098d`; formatting, public-tree and the 189-file asset inventory checks passed.
  Final committed-revision CI and independent release review remain required.
- The 32-client TCP-loopback replication soak converged all clients over 300 ticks.
  Queue-depth p95 was zero; snapshot-age p95 was two ticks; work-time p95 was
  0.62 ms. This measures the replication fixture, not populated-game network AI.
- Twenty serial cold interactive-world loads completed. This local latency
  diagnostic is separate from the paired server performance release gate.
- White ground patches were isolated to water shading: additive caustic light
  clipped over sunlit sand, while broad shoreline foam covered quiet shallows.
  Readback regressions reproduce both faults. Caustic sample normalization,
  modulation of transmitted bed light and broken narrow foam restore visible
  water detail in the complete native ground capture.
- Apparent daytime stars in the ground capture disappear when the ambient mote
  emitter is disabled. They are foreground particles, not the sky star field.
- Purple areas follow the soil material. Independent Astra/xhigh review confirmed
  that pointwise Fresnel and PI-scaled irradiance exaggerated ambient reflection.
  The integrated GGX/Schlick lookup restores brown detailed soil while preserving
  direct lights and the existing diffuse calibration. The initially tried analytic
  fit was rejected after it missed the independent grazing-angle reference.
  A generated 64x64 RG16F table costs 16 KiB and avoids runtime integration.
  Run `python3 tools/rendering/generate_environment_brdf.py --check` to reproduce it.
- Bright storm grass was traced to foliage executing after the weather grade.
  Real foliage/weather readback reproduces the error at opaque and soft-edge pixels.
  Rays now compose before grass, then weather/aerial affect the blended scene.
  A gated snapshot reuses the existing texture for ray-source colors; it adds a copy
  when rays run, not another allocation. A second regression verifies grass alpha
  occludes rays against cleared sky depth. Weather/fog still use background G-buffer
  depth/normals for grass, an approximation at silhouettes against distant scenery.
- Grass also sampled a different cloud field scale and omitted terrain's penumbra.
  Both now use the same projected field and five taps; real production-function
  readback reproduces the old mismatch and verifies the correction.
- An active glass pass regression reproduced an incomplete framebuffer and invisible
  panes. The pass now attaches shared depth as a renderbuffer; resize, render-scale
  changes and cleanup release OIT before its borrowed depth. Visible/occluded panes
  pass at two recreated framebuffer sizes. The same active-pass regression passed natively; full scene capture remains separate.
- The integrated independent review found a second glass error: refraction divided
  internal fragment coordinates by the output extent. At reduced render scales,
  panes sampled the wrong background region. A nonuniform background regression
  reproduces the error at half and quarter scale; using the internal extent passes
  all six pass-context tests on software GL. Native and committed CI follow-up
  remain required.
- Independent high-view isolation attributes the broad outer pastel lift primarily
  to aerial haze. Central/outer soil mean RGB changed from approximately
  138/111/107 versus 150/126/123 to 123/91/87 versus 123/91/88 without aerial.
  Raw albedo matched closely; normals retain less fine detail outside. These screen
  regions are illustrative, not exact residency masks. Individual cool pixels do
  not establish a material-binding defect. Haze tuning and detail continuity remain
  explicit authoring/longer-view work; no unsupported palette correction was applied.

Native Default/424242 captures use FOV 110 degrees and the recorded ground,
high-altitude and cave-air poses. They have zero generation, meshing and terrain
upload backlog. The six three-frame timing samples are diagnostics, not a forest
budget verdict. The separate forest pose measured 240 frames after 400 warmup:
15.14 ms mean frame wall time and 6.04 ms summed GPU pass timers. This is the
composed Default scene, not the synthetic 16,000-tree stress fixture. CPU-submit
timing includes an unexplained residual and does not establish a hardware bottleneck.
The final runtime pack reports 16,777,200 bytes of static-model textures and
169,869,312 bytes of tree impostor textures; the forest capture reports 678,945,120
estimated resource bytes, including the 16,384-byte BRDF lookup. These estimates
are not measured total VRAM. The native executable was built before the changes
were committed as `657c731`; exact final version-stamped packaging remains required.

CI on `9fa0e11` passed 1,975 of 1,976 tests on Linux, ASan and MSVC; the sole
failure was the stale public asset inventory. The inventory reads indexed Git blobs
and had been regenerated before changed assets were staged. It is now regenerated
from the published bytes. Static analysis also required checked optional access and
non-owning stop-token parameters in the catalog; those corrections preserve validation.
Paired server/water artifacts at `9fa0e11` have matching comparability keys, 20
samples per side and evaluated stable verdicts against `7014c984`: server wall-time
median +0.22%, water workload +1.19%, and water-phase p95 metric -2.10%. These remain
baseline evidence until the final candidate and promotion gates complete.

Independent Astra/xhigh review of the integrated `657c731` candidate found the
glass render-scale defect above and no additional demonstrated P0-P2 defects in
its examined scope. It independently reran 13 acquisition and 17 performance
fixture tests, regenerated the BRDF lookup, compared 32 numerical quadrature
samples, and checked archive/member, source-provenance, saved/refused-world and
JUnit evidence. This source/evidence review is not final release approval.

## Remaining release gates and limits

Final committed-candidate checks, applicable native/manual coverage, the unchanged
120-second UCRT64 guard, server-load qualification, paired performance gates,
independent Astra/xhigh verification, strict CI promotion, signed source-only
publication and independent release-download verification remain required.

Finite surface residency, the 3,200 m far plane, finite cave residency and omitted
distant caves/edits remain visible limitations. Cave captures still expose the
finite cave boundary. Audio audibility/event coverage, broader hardware and real
GNS/Steam transport qualification must not be inferred from decoding, software GL
or compile probes. The original intermittent server-load crash retains its own
reproduction and unresolved cause; the separate MSVC exception-configuration fix
does not establish its cause.

See the [roadmap](roadmap.md) and its individual acceptance issues for ownership,
dependencies and closure requirements. Engine/game extraction follows verified
publication and the agreed roadmap approval.
